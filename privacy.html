Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 25, 2026

The NARBE Foundation (“NARBE Foundation,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy of the families, caregivers, and individuals we serve. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at info@narbefoundation.org.

Who We Are

The NARBE Foundation is a nonprofit organization dedicated to expanding access to assistive technology, including our switch kit donation program and Benny’s Accessibility Hub. We are a separate entity from NARBE House and NARBE LLC.

Information We Collect

Switch Kit Donation Program

When you apply for or receive a switch kit through our donation program, we collect:

  • Your name (and the name of the recipient, if different)
  • Email address
  • Mailing address (needed to ship the kit)
  • Phone number
  • Any additional information you choose to share with us in the application form, such as details about the recipient’s needs

Information submitted through our Google Form is stored temporarily in Google Drive and Google Sheets for the purpose of processing and fulfilling your request. Access is restricted to authorized NARBE Foundation personnel only.

Emails, Messages, and Inquiries

When you email us, fill out a contact form on our website, message us on social media (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, or other platforms), or otherwise reach out to us, we receive whatever information you choose to share. This often includes:

  • Your name and contact information
  • Your relationship to the person you are inquiring about
  • Details about your loved one’s diagnosis, abilities, or care needs
  • Questions, stories, or experiences you choose to share with us

We understand that families often share deeply personal information when reaching out, and we treat these communications with care and respect.

We keep email correspondence as long as it is helpful to us in serving you. Having that history helps us understand your situation and provide better support if you reach out again. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Photos, Videos, and Stories

Families sometimes share photos, videos, or written stories with us through email, social media, or as part of switch kit follow-up. We do not share, post, or use this content publicly without your clear permission.

There are a few ways permission can be given:

  • Tagging us on social media. When families tag the NARBE Foundation in a post, we may repost or share that content on our channels.
  • Signed permission forms. In some cases, we ask families to complete and sign a release form when uploading content, which includes your agreement to let us share it.
  • Direct request from us. If we would like to feature your story or photo on NARBE Foundation channels and one of the above does not apply, we will reach out first and confirm what you are comfortable with.

You may request that a post or piece of media be removed at any time by emailing us directly at info@narbefoundation.org. We will do our best to take down the content, but please note that some materials, such as newsletters or printed outreach that have already been sent out with your permission, may not be possible to fully retract.

Benny’s Accessibility Hub

When you use Benny’s Accessibility Hub, we collect very limited, anonymous usage statistics, including:

  • That a visitor is using the Hub
  • The country the visitor is connecting from

We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses tied to identity, or any personal information through the Hub. These stats simply help us understand how the Hub is being used so we can keep it running and improve it.

Website Visitors and Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors find and use it. Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • General geographic location (country or region, not a precise address)
  • The type of device and browser you are using
  • How you arrived at our site (for example, from a search engine or a social media link)

This information is collected in aggregate and anonymized. We use it only to improve our website and better understand the needs of our community. Google Analytics data is governed by Google’s own privacy policy. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Donations

Donations to the NARBE Foundation are processed by Givebutter, a third-party fundraising platform. When you donate, you provide your information directly to Givebutter’s payment forms. Depending on what you choose to enter, Givebutter may collect your name, email address, mailing address, donation amount, and payment details.

We never see or store your card or bank account numbers. The NARBE Foundation receives only the donor and donation records that Givebutter reports to us, which we use to acknowledge your gift, issue tax receipts, and meet nonprofit recordkeeping requirements. See Donor Privacy below for how we handle that information.

Newsletter Signups

Our newsletter is delivered through MailerLite. When you subscribe, we collect the name and email address you provide. MailerLite also records standard email engagement information, such as whether a message was opened or a link was clicked, which helps us understand whether our updates are useful. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email we send.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Some of the services we use to run this website set cookies or use similar technologies. Here is what is running on narbefoundation.org and why:

  • Google Analytics. Sets cookies that help us count visitors and understand which pages are useful. These are analytics cookies, not advertising cookies. We do not run ads and we do not use Google Analytics for ad targeting or remarketing.
  • MailerLite. Loads on pages that offer our newsletter signup and may set a cookie to remember whether you have already seen or dismissed a signup form, so we are not asking you over and over.
  • Givebutter. Loads on our donation pages and sets cookies needed to run the donation form securely and to keep your donation session working correctly.

We do not use advertising, retargeting, or social media tracking pixels on this website. We do not sell or share any of this information.

Your choices about cookies

  • Browser settings. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking them will not prevent you from reading this site, though the donation form may not work correctly without its cookies.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics. Google offers a browser add-on that opts you out of Google Analytics on every site you visit: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Our website does not track you across other websites, so there is nothing for these signals to switch off here. We also honor them in spirit: we will not add cross-site tracking to this website.

Accessibility Settings Stored in Your Browser

Our website includes an accessibility toolbar that lets you change things like text size, contrast, and the OpenDyslexic font. So that you do not have to set your preferences again on every page, we save them in your own browser’s local storage under the name narbe-accessibility-prefs.

These settings never leave your device. They are not sent to the NARBE Foundation, they are not shared with anyone, and they are not used to identify or track you. You can erase them at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for narbefoundation.org.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you give us only for the purpose you provided it. Specifically:

  • To review and process switch kit applications
  • To ship switch kits and follow up with recipients
  • To check in with switch kit recipients about how things are going and how we can improve our program
  • To answer questions you send us
  • To acknowledge donations and issue tax receipts
  • To keep records required for nonprofit compliance and reporting
  • To improve our programs and the resources we offer

If you applied for a switch kit, please note that the application itself lets you know we may follow up with you after your kit is delivered. That follow-up helps us understand how the kit is being used and how we can better support your family.

How We Protect Your Information

Protecting the information of the families we serve is a top priority. Here’s how we handle it:

  1. Short-term storage in Google Drive and Google Sheets. While a switch kit application is being processed, the form responses live in a secure, access-restricted Google Drive and Google Sheets owned by the NARBE Foundation. Only authorized Foundation personnel have access.
  2. Long-term storage offline. Once an application has been processed, we transfer the records from Google Drive to an external hard drive that is not connected to the internet. This significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access. The original copies in Google Drive are then deleted.
  3. Access is limited. Only NARBE Foundation personnel who need the information to do their work can access it.
  4. Sensitive health and personal information. We recognize that families often share medical, developmental, or other sensitive information when applying for a switch kit or reaching out to us. While the NARBE Foundation is not a medical or healthcare provider and is not bound by HIPAA, we treat this information with the same care we would want for our own family. We do not share it outside the Foundation, and we keep it only as long as needed to serve you.
  5. No payment information. We do not collect or store credit card or banking information through our forms. Donations made to the Foundation are handled by trusted third-party payment processors with their own security practices.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not rent, trade, or share your information with marketers or data brokers.
  • We do not share your information with third parties for promotional purposes.
  • We do not publicly post your name, contact information, or story without your clear, written permission. We often have forms where you can agree to share information, and doing so is always optional.
  • We do not run advertising, retargeting, or social media tracking pixels on this website.
  • We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined under state privacy laws, and we never have.

Donor Privacy

Donors are entitled to know how their information will be treated before they give. This section applies to everyone who donates to the NARBE Foundation.

  • We never sell, rent, trade, or share our donor list. Not with other nonprofits, not with marketers, not with anyone.
  • We use donor information only for Foundation purposes: processing your gift, sending your receipt and acknowledgment, thanking you, keeping the records the IRS requires of us, and letting you know how your support is being used.
  • You can give anonymously. If you would prefer that your name not appear in any Foundation materials, tell us at info@narbefoundation.org and we will honor that.
  • We will not publish your name, gift amount, or story without your permission.
  • You can opt out of donor communications at any time while still remaining a donor. Being on our mailing list is never a condition of giving.
  • Payment details never reach us. Card and bank information is handled entirely by Givebutter and its payment processors.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your information only as long as we need it.

  • Switch kit applications: Once your kit has been delivered and any follow-up is complete, we move your information from Google Drive to our offline hard drive. We keep these records for as long as needed for nonprofit recordkeeping and compliance, and to follow up with you about future programs if you have opted in.
  • Emails and inquiries: We keep email correspondence for as long as it is helpful in serving you. Having that history gives us context if you reach out again, so we can better understand your situation and pick up where we left off. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.
  • Benny’s Accessibility Hub stats: Anonymous usage stats are kept only in summary form to help us understand how the Hub is being used over time.
  • Donation records: We keep donation records for as long as required by IRS and state nonprofit reporting rules.
  • Newsletter subscriptions: We keep your subscription information until you unsubscribe, after which MailerLite retains a suppression record so we do not accidentally email you again.

If you would like us to delete your information sooner, just ask.

Emails and Newsletters

You may receive emails from the NARBE Foundation, such as newsletters, updates, upcoming event announcements, and messages intended to support and inform you.

You may receive emails because you:

  • Signed up for our newsletter through our website
  • Sent us an email asking for help or with an inquiry
  • Made a donation to the Foundation
  • Signed up for a program or event we hosted

You may unsubscribe at any time using the “Unsubscribe” link at the bottom of any newsletter or email campaign sent by the Foundation.

When We May Share Information

We will only share your information in these limited situations:

  • With your permission. For example, if you agree to share your story or be featured in NARBE Foundation outreach.
  • With service providers we rely on to operate. These are listed by name in Third-Party Services We Use above. When we ship a switch kit, the recipient’s name and mailing address are shared with the carrier as a necessary part of fulfillment. These providers only receive what they need to do their job.
  • With NARBE LLC in limited cases that directly support our programs. NARBE LLC builds and maintains the games and tools available through Benny’s Accessibility Hub. If a family shares feedback about how a switch kit recipient is using those resources, that feedback may be shared with NARBE LLC so they can improve accessibility and update the free tools the Foundation provides. Personal information from applications and inquiries is not shared for any promotional or commercial purpose.
  • If required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request, court order, or to comply with nonprofit reporting requirements.

Children’s Privacy

Many of the individuals who benefit from our switch kit program are minors with disabilities. In those cases, we collect information from a parent, guardian, or caregiver, not directly from the child. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental or guardian involvement.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Benny’s Accessibility Hub is designed to be used by children and collects no personal information at all from anyone who uses it. Our website is directed to parents, guardians, caregivers, educators, and supporters rather than to children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@narbefoundation.org and we will delete it promptly.

When parents or guardians choose to share photos, videos, or stories about a minor with us, we treat that information with extra care and never share it publicly without explicit written permission from the parent or guardian.

Your Choices and Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what information we have about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your information (except where we are required to keep it for legal or compliance reasons)
  • Withdraw your application at any time
  • Ask us to stop emailing you, without affecting anything else

To make any of these requests, email us at info@narbefoundation.org. We will respond within a reasonable time. We will never charge you for making a request, and we will never treat you differently for making one.

If Something Goes Wrong

We take protecting your information seriously, but no system is perfect. If we ever discover that personal information has been accessed without permission or otherwise compromised, we will:

  • Take immediate steps to secure the information
  • Notify affected individuals as soon as reasonably possible, and in accordance with applicable Rhode Island law
  • Comply with any legal reporting obligations

Visitors From Outside the United States

The NARBE Foundation is based in the United States, and any information we collect is stored and processed here. If you are visiting Benny’s Accessibility Hub or contacting us from outside the U.S., please know that your information will be handled in accordance with this policy and U.S. law.

Accessibility of This Policy

We serve families who rely on assistive technology, so a privacy policy that is hard to read would be a contradiction. This page is written in plain language, works with screen readers and keyboard navigation, responds to your browser’s text size settings, and can be adjusted with the accessibility toolbar on this site.

If any part of this policy is difficult for you to read or understand, tell us at info@narbefoundation.org and we will provide it in another format or explain it to you directly. We mean that.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll update the “Effective Date” at the top of this page. If we make significant changes, we’ll do our best to let our community know.
